Q2

Which kind of connected account? Partly answered

Found: Stripe marks its Accounts v1 docs and the legacy "Standard" account type as deprecated for new platforms. That information now applies only to platforms already using the legacy types, and the plan chose Standard.

To check: how a new platform configures a connected account today, and which setup gives what the plan wants: the vendor owns a full Stripe dashboard, charges are direct, and Stripe handles verification, payouts and disputes. Then recheck R3's two facts under it: that we can prefill the account, and that verification details lock once the first onboarding link exists.

Matters for R3, Q9 and Q12 · Read: Connect account types, Standard accounts (legacy)

Q3

How long a card hold lasts Answered

Found: 7 days for an online card payment the customer starts (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover). If the hold expires uncaptured, the funds are released and the payment's status becomes canceled. Each charge says exactly when its hold ends (capture_before), and longer holds exist for some payments on request.

So: a same-day order is nowhere near the limit. The rest of Q3, the confirmation timeout against the market day's end, is ours to decide.

Matters for steps 2, 3 and 5 · Read: Place a hold on a payment method

Q4

What Checkout does against card testing Partly answered

Found: on Checkout, Stripe applies its own rate limits, fraud models and CAPTCHA automatically, and labels what it stops "Blocked by Stripe". How well it works depends on the risk signals the integration sends. Stripe also recommends merchant-side measures: rate limits per IP, a CAPTCHA, and Radar rules.

To check: how this applies to a direct charge on the vendor's own account: whose Radar runs, and who sees what was blocked.

Matters for step 2 · Read: Card testing, How do I prevent card testing attacks?

Q5

When a capture is refused for good Partly answered

Found: an expired hold turns the payment to canceled (as in Q3).

To check: which webhook reports that change (we expect payment_intent.canceled), and what a capture call returns when it's refused, and whether any event follows. R2 needs a webhook for every outcome; if a refused capture sends none, the processor has to record the failure itself.

Matters for step 3a and R2 · Read: Place a hold on a payment method, API reference (PaymentIntents, capture; Events, types)

Q10

How long an idempotency key lasts Answered

Found: keys may be pruned once they are at least 24 hours old (API v1); a key reused after that runs as a new request. Stripe compares a repeated request's parameters with the original's and errors if they differ. Requests that fail validation, or collide with a concurrent one, aren't saved and can be retried.

So: the order ID as the key is safe for a same-day order.

Matters for R2 · Read: Idempotent requests

Q12

Does the vendor confirm prefilled details? Open

Found, for the legacy types: on a Standard account, the platform can prefill any information when it creates the account, including the representative as a person; once the first onboarding link exists, it can no longer read or write verification details. On an Express account, onboarding doesn't ask again for what was prefilled, but has the account holder confirm it before accepting Stripe's terms.

To check: the same two behaviours under the current model (Q2).

Matters for step 1 and R3 · Read: Standard accounts, Express accounts

Q13

Linking a Stripe account the vendor already has Partly answered

Found: OAuth can still link an existing Standard account, but Stripe doesn't recommend it for new platforms and points them to its own onboarding instead. Since June 2021, a platform using OAuth with full access can't link a Standard account another platform controls.

So: expect most vendors to open a new Stripe account through our onboarding, even if they already use Stripe elsewhere.

To check: whether the current model lets a vendor reuse an existing account at all.

Matters for step 1 · Read: OAuth with Standard accounts, OAuth changes for Standard platforms
